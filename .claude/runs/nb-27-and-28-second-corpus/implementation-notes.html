NB-27 & NB-28 — the decomposition, and the second corpus

Branches nb-27-differential-decomposition and nb-28-second-corpus · 2026-08-22 · amendments 5 and 6 to protocol 003.

The spec, in one line

Ask which arm produced prediction A's differential (amendment 5, post-hoc), then register a prediction on a second corpus and score it (amendment 6, pre-registered). Both amendments tagged; amendment 6 tagged before any 2Wiki arm ran.

Design decisions

Decision The decomposition is its own artifact. run() returns three things and decomposition.json is written separately, with family_size held at 12. A post-hoc statistic sitting inside analysis.json is how it gets read as registered.
Decision analysis2wiki.py is a separate module, and quotes HotpotQA rather than recomputing it. Parameterising analysis.py by corpus would put two published runs on one code path where a change made for the second could silently move the first. HotpotQA's figures are constants here, so this module cannot alter them.
Decision Predictions stated on the arm's own between-class difference. Amendment 5 showed a differential between two arms cannot attribute the differential. Stating C in the old shape would have made the follow-up unfalsifiable the same way.

Deviations

Deviation 2Wiki scores 9,825 questions, not 12,576. Its 2,751 bridge_comparison questions carry four gold documents, where R@2 caps at 0.50. Pooling them would make the primary measure mean a different thing on each corpus. Frozen in the amendment before scoring, applied identically to all four arms.
Deviation One text per title, by majority variant. 1,242 titles (2.9%) carry more than one text — whitespace artifacts, median similarity 0.994. HotpotQA's pool raised on any title collision; 2Wiki cannot meet that bar, so the weaker guarantee is stated rather than the check quietly dropped.

Tradeoffs

Tradeoff Predicted a 10-point shrink; got 9.12. A rounder threshold would have flipped D to supported. It was registered before the run and it stays refuted. Moving it afterwards is the whole failure mode this sequence documents.

Open questions

Open Is the extractor or the mechanism the binding constraint? The arm cannot link 41.1% of 2Wiki queries to any node. Until experiment 004 swaps the extractor on both corpora, "this arm cannot traverse" and "graphs built this way cannot traverse" are not separated.
Open The coverage-0 stratum is missing from 2Wiki. One query, against HotpotQA's 1,821. The classic bridge contrast survives; the hardest case does not. See corrections C9 — the spec had said so and the amendment did not carry it forward.
